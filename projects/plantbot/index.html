PlantBot

A versioned engineering journal following one small system from physical measurement, through software decisions, to eventual physical actuation.

PlantBot is currently at the **planning, procurement, and learning** stage. I started it to work across electronics, sensors, embedded programming, data collection, power, and control logic without hiding the unfinished parts of the process. The long-term idea is modular plant monitoring and eventually simple watering automation; no physical prototype or working watering system exists yet. ## Current direction The first build is deliberately narrow: one plant, one capacitive soil-moisture sensor, one ESP32, one pump, simple wiring, and basic logging. I want to understand each layer individually before adding more sensors, networking, dashboards, or sophisticated control. ## Development Log
